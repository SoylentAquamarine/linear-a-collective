## Falsification and uncertainty
A reader with repository/atlas access can recover the confidence tier for every packet entry, so repository-level blinding fails. A packet-only external reader can still be blind if a separate coordinator distributes only the CSV and frozen rubric, without repository links or atlas access. The tier counts do not establish phonetic correctness. The reading hypothesis must be tested against held-out inscriptions and competing language/system models; failure to generalize or dependence on Linear B look-alikes falsifies it.
