## Falsification and uncertainty
This reproduces a data join, not phonetic correctness, language identification or a reading. A reader with repository/atlas access can recover every confidence tier, so repository-level blinding fails. A packet-only external reader can still be blind if a separate coordinator provides only the frozen CSV and rubric and withholds the atlas/repository. A language hypothesis must generalize to held-out inscriptions and outperform competing language/system models; failure to generalize or reliance on Linear B look-alikes falsifies it.
